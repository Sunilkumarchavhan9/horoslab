## 6. Scientific Limitations & Formal Non-Claims

To maintain total scientific integrity, the following boundaries are formally established:

1. **No Claim of Certified Safety:** While zero safety violations were observed across 30 episodes in $C_1$ ($0/30$), this yields an empirical 95% Wilson confidence upper bound of $11.35\%$. We do not claim certified safety for unconstrained neural MPC.
2. **Task 1 Incomplete Seating:** The corrected reward successfully unlocked chamber entry (achieving 8.04 mm penetration), but the peg did not reach the seating criterion ($z \le -50.0\text{ mm}$). Kinematic analysis indicates $H=25$–$40$ steps is insufficient for compliant contact insertion; horizon sweeps up to $H=150$ are required.
3. **Decoupled Safety Filter Limit Cycle:** The baseline p-CBF safety filter is fundamentally decoupled from the planner. Clamping shear commands without compensating normal preload creates a deadband where the robot repeatedly stalls.

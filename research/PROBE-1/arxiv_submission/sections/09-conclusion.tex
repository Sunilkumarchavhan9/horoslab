\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented HOROS PROBE-1, a predictive Control Barrier Function safety framework with Adaptive Conformal Inference for safe multi-fingered robotic grasping under coupled nonstationary contact conditions. By coupling linearized Hunt-Crossley elastomeric dynamics with an 8-sided polyhedral friction cone inner approximation and grasp matrix wrench balance, the proposed controller provides real-time proactive force modulation within an embedded quadratic program. 

Across 3,400 independent simulation trials in Isaac Lab with PhysX 5 continuous collision physics, HOROS achieved a \textbf{0.0\% drop rate and 0.0\% crush rate} under coupled nonstationarity, completely outperforming classical fixed-force, reactive reflex, unconstrained flow matching, and certainty-equivalence MPC baselines (all suffering 100\% drop rates, $p < 10^{-20}$). Systematic 7-component ablations proved that slip prevention and structural object integrity are competing physical objectives that require two-sided barrier constraints coupled with full proprioceptive kinematics. The embedded C-level QP solver executes with a median latency of \textbf{0.193~ms}, satisfying hard real-time deadlines at 100~Hz. Future research will explore embedding this predictive safety filter as an inductive bias directly within diffusion-based foundation manipulation policies.

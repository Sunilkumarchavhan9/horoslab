grade its own outcome.

1 import verifiers as vf

AUTHORITY & TRUST[12/13]

Authority, proof, and trust for autonomous organizations.

Every agent, human, tool, budget, verifier, and policy acts inside a governed institutional boundary.

Principle
No Ambient Authority
Authority is never inherited implicitly. Every human, agent, and institution begins with zero privileges. Every capability must be explicitly granted through institutional policy, eliminating hidden trust paths and ensuring every consequential action has accountable authorization.
Principle

Principle
Mission-Scoped Access
Authority is bound to a mission, not an identity. Capability Leases grant only the precise permissions required to accomplish an approved objective, automatically enforcing least privilege across repositories, infrastructure, APIs, and enterprise systems.
Principle

Principle
Short-Lived Capability Leases
Permissions are leased, never owned. Every capability is issued with defined scope, purpose, and expiration, then automatically revoked when the mission completes. Long-lived credentials disappear in favor of temporary, verifiable authority.
